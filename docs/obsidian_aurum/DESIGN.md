## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier digital identity ecosystem positioned at the intersection of cultural prestige, research rigor, and frontier computing. It conveys an editorial, cinematic, and authoritative atmosphere tailored for intellectual leadership, innovative case archives, and high-impact personal identity platforms. 

The emotional response is calculated: deep, focused stillness paired with deliberate moments of luminous precision. The visual language rejects SaaS homogeneity, decorative skeuomorphism, and neon-saturated cyberpunk tropes. Instead, it fuses brutalist structural discipline with hyper-refined luxury editorial aesthetics. Space is treated as a physical medium—expansive, dark, and purposeful—allowing typographic craft, hairline structures, and directional photonic illumination to command focus.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict dark color mode, defined by near-absolute deep darks layered against warm editorial paper whites and dual metaphysical accents.

- **Primary (`#C8A96B` — Champagne / Muted Gold):** Encapsulates heritage, academic tenure, cultural gravity, and leadership. Applied selectively to focal typographic highlights, honors, status pills, and primary CTA accents. It is never over-saturated or metallic-glossed; it remains matte, architectural, and warm.
- **Secondary (`#7C7AFF` — Electric Indigo / Subtle Violet):** Represents frontier technological exploration, computational research, AI integration, and scientific synthesis. Utilized for terminal prompts, telemetry signals, interactive research tags, and machine-intelligence states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#08090B` Foundation, `#111318` Surface, `#17191F` Elevated):** A warm-tinted obsidian spectrum providing deep, quiet optical contrast while eradicating raw OLED eye fatigue.
- **Typographic Pairings:** `#F5F3EE` delivers an unbleached parchment luminance for primary legibility, while `#9B9DA5` balances ancillary data, research abstracts, and metadata without competing for visual hierarchy.
- **Structural Hairlines (`#272A31`):** A razor-sharp, low-contrast boundary system maintaining architectural integrity across modular panels and section thresholds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances dramatic editorial flair, ergonomic readability, and machine-level catalog precision through three deliberate type families:

1. **Editorial Display (`Syne`):** Used for impactful titles, case study titles, and branding moments. Its sculpted structural geometry projects uncompromising artistic vision, intellectual courage, and forward velocity.
2. **Reading & Structural Prose (`Space Grotesk`):** Bridges modern geometric construction with humanist clarity. It handles multi-paragraph project narratives, research theses, and navigational wayfinding without fatigue.
3. **Data, Metrics & Micro-Interface (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical metadata, dates, indexing numbers (e.g., `01 // ARCHIVE`), DOI references, and interactive agent terminals. All micro-labels are typographically tracking-expanded and formatted with tabular numeric data.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column editorial grid. Rather than packing content into monotonous card collections, the system employs deliberate rhythm and alternating negative spaces to mirror publication design.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop Large` (≥1440px): 12 columns, 64px section margins, generous lateral whitespace gutters.
  - `Desktop` (1024px–1439px): 12 columns, 48px section margins, fluid column widths.
  - `Tablet` (768px–1023px): 8 columns, 32px margins, dynamic vertical reflow for asymmetrical blocks.
  - `Mobile` (<768px): 4 columns, 20px margins, single-column vertical linear stack with sticky persistent metadata ribbons.

- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  Vertical spacing between macro sections operates on an expansive rhythm (64px to 128px) to provide pacing between distinct intellectual domains (Identity, Research, Platform Architecture). Internal component padding adheres strictly to the defined rem scale to preserve pixel alignment and consistent density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through chromatic tonal layering, structural hairline containment, and soft optical illumination rather than conventional diffuse shadows.

- **Tonal Stepping:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#08090B` serves as the infinite spatial backdrop.
  - **Level 1 (Structural Surfaces):** `#111318` houses content modules, structural sidebars, and inactive case panels.
  - **Level 2 (Active/Elevated Containers):** `#17191F` surfaces dialogs, popovers, active selection states, and floating control anchors.

- **Hairline Boundaries:** Every surface separation is declared by a 1px solid stroke of `#272A31`, guaranteeing razor-sharp legibility even on low-luminosity screens.

- **Photonic Illumination & Micro-Glows:**
  No heavy drop shadows are permitted. Depth is instead established via low-intensity radial atmospheric backlights:
  - Leadership & Heritage components project a directional, blurred backlight: `radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.08) 0%, transparent 70%)`.
  - Research, Compute, and AI interfaces utilize a focused indigo emission: `radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(124, 122, 255, 0.12) 0%, transparent 60%)`.
  - Focused interactive elements introduce an inner hairline highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(245, 243, 238, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by a `Soft` (Level 1) roundedness strategy. The system decisively avoids playful rounded pills or blunt, sharp edges, favoring architectural refinement:

- **Standard Base Components (Buttons, Inputs, Tags, Badges):** 0.25rem (4px) corner radius.
- **Structural Surfaces (Cards, Modals, Flyout Panels):** 0.5rem (8px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Interactive Badges & Dynamic Status Pills:** Pill geometries are strictly prohibited for primary containers and restricted exclusively to technical status tags or circular icon touch-targets.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Defined strictly with hairline 1px strokes, clean 90-degree vector terminations, and zero beveling.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Champagne Luxury):** Background `#C8A96B`, text `#08090B` (`Space Grotesk`, semi-bold). On hover: shifts toward `#D8BE8A` with a subtle 0.25rem translate-y lift.
- **Secondary / Tech (Indigo Interactive):** Hairline border `#7C7AFF`, background `rgba(124, 122, 255, 0.06)`, text `#F5F3EE`. On hover: border `#9795FF`, background `rgba(124, 122, 255, 0.14)`.
- **Ghost / Editorial:** Transparent background, hairline border `#272A31`, text `#F5F3EE`. On hover: border `#9B9DA5`, background `#17191F`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Container:** Background `#111318`, hairline border 1px `#272A31`, radius 0.25rem, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Input value `#F5F3EE`, placeholder `#9B9DA5` (`Space Grotesk`).
- **Focus State:** 1px stroke `#C8A96B` or `#7C7AFF` (context-dependent) accompanied by a 2px outer aura glow `rgba(124, 122, 255, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Base Canvas:** `#111318` background, 1px `#272A31` perimeter border, 0.5rem corner radius.
- **Content Padding:** `space-lg` (2rem) interior clearance.
- **Interaction:** On pointer hover, the outer hairline transitions from `#272A31` to `#C8A96B` at 200ms ease, paired with a faint interior top illumination.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Technical Tag:** Font `JetBrains Mono`, 11px uppercase, tracking `0.12em`. Background `#17191F`, hairline border `#272A31`, text `#9B9DA5`.
- **Research Highlight Tag:** Text `#7C7AFF`, background `rgba(124, 122, 255, 0.08)`, border `rgba(124, 122, 255, 0.24)`.
- **Heritage Status Badge:** Text `#C8A96B`, background `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.08)`, border `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.24)`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Box Frame:** 16px x 16px square with 2px radius, background `#111318`, border 1px `#272A31`.
- **Selected State:** Fill `#C8A96B`, check icon `#08090B`.

### Lists & Archival Index Records
- **Structure:** Clean tabular horizontal dividers with hairline `#272A31`.
- **Typography:** Index counters in `JetBrains Mono` (`#9B9DA5`), title in `Syne` (`#F5F3EE`), metadata right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Row Interaction:** On row hover, subtle shift to `#17191F` background with an active gold indicator bar (2px width) on the left margin.

### Persistent Floating AI Agent Trigger
- **Form Factor:** 48px circular or 4px soft-radius trigger anchored at bottom-right viewport.
- **Materiality:** Deep surface `#17191F`, hairline border `#7C7AFF`, housing a radiant spark icon with an ambient indigo pulse.
